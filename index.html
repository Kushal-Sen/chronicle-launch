You are invited to the inaugural edition

For far too long, men's struggles have gone unheard. All Bengal Men's Forum is proud to launch The Men's Rights Chronicle, a newsletter to break the silence, challenge stereotypes, encourage informed dialogue and advocate meaningful legal and social reforms.

Join us for the release of Volume 1, Issue 01, on 15 November 2026 at 8:00 PM IST. Inside:

  • The President's message, by MRA Nandini Bhattacharjee
  • Words of welcome from Dr. Jerome Teelucksingh and Halima Layeni
  • Lead story: the Walk for Men's Commission on 22 November
  • What the law says on domestic violence
  • Dates for your diary, 19 to 22 November, and the ABMF helpline

It is free for distribution. Please read it, share it, and bring someone with you.

Justice has no gender. Human dignity has no boundaries.
